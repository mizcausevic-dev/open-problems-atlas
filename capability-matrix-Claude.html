Provenance

How 591 problems get here

Four stages, each with the bug it produced before it worked. Amber text is what went wrong.

01 · FETCH

MediaWiki API

218 KB of wikitext plus the revision ID, which is recorded so a reader can diff against the source.

02 · NORMALISE

Collapse and mask

Citations and templates wrap across lines; the list structure is line-based.

Multi-line refs leaked <ref> into descriptions until newlines inside them were collapsed first.
03 · PARSE

Bullets to entries

Headings give the field, nesting gives parent links, the trailing parenthetical gives solver and year.

Sub-bullets were skipped at first — which dropped the Riemann Hypothesis, a ** item.
04 · VERIFY

18 dataset tests

Markup residue, balanced $, valid parent links, all seven Millennium problems, no invented metrics.

Caught an entry titled "Aleksandar Nikolov" — the person who settled it, not the problem.
Conflicting listings are kept as explicit variants rather than merged. The Jacobian conjecture is open in two dimensions and was disproven above two in 2026; blending those into one status would state something the source does not.
